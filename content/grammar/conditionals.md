## Условные предложения

> [!info] Zero Conditional — универсальные истины
> **If + Present Simple, Present Simple**
> *If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.*

> [!tip] First Conditional — реальное будущее
> **If + Present Simple, will + V1**
> *If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home.*

> [!example] Second Conditional — маловероятное настоящее/будущее
> **If + Past Simple, would + V1**
> *If I were rich, I would travel around the world.*
>
> Все лица: *I were, he were, they were* (в формальном английском)

> [!warning] Third Conditional — нереальное прошлое
> **If + Past Perfect, would have + V3**
> *If I had studied harder, I would have passed the exam.*
